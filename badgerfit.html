The Product

BadgerFit.

A foot-scanning and shoe fit-check tool that helps you understand your feet and make more informed footwear decisions.

Common Fit Problems

Built for people dealing with recurring footwear discomfort.

01

Toe pressure, heel slipping, side pressure, end-of-day foot fatigue, discomfort after activity, inconsistent sizing and uncertainty about width, cushioning or insoles.

Two Entry Points

Scan versus Fit Check.

02
Foot Scan

A guided, A4-referenced top-view scan producing estimated measurements and sizing guidance. Requires paper, camera and account.

Fit Check

A question-based assessment of one specific uncomfortable shoe. No scan or paper required. Account required.

Scan Requirements

What you'll need.

03

One sheet of A4 paper, a phone camera, adequate lighting, and a BadgerFit account. The camera-guidance process runs a live checklist for paper visibility, foot placement, lighting and steadiness before capture.

Results

What you get back.

04

Estimated foot length and width, a footprint-based arch estimate, a suggested UK/India shoe size, an insert-size category, and a beta Badger Cluster grouping. Plus rule-based comfort recommendations and, if you skip the scan, Quick Relief/Fit Check guidance without either.

Current Limitations

What this is not.

06

The scan is not a 3D scan, a medical scan, a gait assessment or a pressure assessment. It does not diagnose injuries and does not directly measure foot volume, instep girth or heel width. It uses classical computer vision, not 3D landmark detection. Fit Check is deterministic and rule-based — not an AI chatbot, and not a medical diagnosis.

FAQs

Common questions.

07
Do I need an account?
Yes, both the foot scan and Fit Check currently require you to sign in.
Is this a medical device?
No. BadgerFit provides educational fit guidance, not a medical diagnosis.
What is a Badger Cluster?
A beta grouping based on available width and arch information. Its boundaries are still being evaluated — it is not yet a validated manufacturing standard.
Will this lead to Badger footwear?
That's the long-term direction, but it depends on validated data and evidence. Nothing is available for sale today.
