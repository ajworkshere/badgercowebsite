Product Preview

What Badger Fit is being built to help with.

Badger Fit is a pre-launch product concept focused on helping people understand fit, recognise what their footwear may be doing wrong, and make better decisions before the next purchase.

Pre-launch, not live.

This page explains the planned experience in honest terms. It is here to help visitors understand the product before launch, not to pretend the app is already available.

Two Entry Points

Badger Fit is being shaped around two starting points.

01
Understand your feet

The future scan path is intended to help people build clearer context around their own feet, rather than relying only on a size label.

Understand one uncomfortable shoe

The future Fit Check path is intended to help people focus on one shoe problem at a time and make sense of what to try next.

Inside the Experience

What the future product may help people see.

02
Foot Context

Understand your fit profile.

Badger Fit is being designed to turn raw fit questions into clearer context around measurements, fit patterns, and what may matter in footwear choice.

One Shoe Problem

Get a focused Fit Check plan.

The product concept includes a path for one uncomfortable shoe at a time, so the next step can feel practical instead of generic.

Education

Learn the language of fit.

Relevant explanations are meant to sit alongside the product experience so people can better understand why footwear can feel wrong.

Interface visuals are illustrative and pre-launch. They explain the concept rather than confirm a finished released product.

What to Expect

Transparent product context.

Purpose
Help people understand fit and footwear needs in plain language before the next decision or purchase.
Approach
Educational guidance, not a medical diagnosis and not a replacement for professional care.

Questions People May Have

Straight answers before launch.

04
Is Badger Fit live today?
No. The product is still pre-launch, and the current site is focused on explaining the concept and collecting early interest through the waitlist.
What will Fit Check mean on this site?
Fit Check refers to the future product path for understanding one uncomfortable shoe. It is product information here, not a separate live page or released tool.
Will Badger Fit diagnose medical issues?
No. The planned experience is educational and fit-focused, not a medical diagnosis.
