The Mechanics

How It Works.

Two journeys through BadgerFit — one starting with a scan, one starting with a specific shoe problem.

Journey One

The Foot Scan.

01
A4 Reference→ Guided Image→ Perspective Correction→ Estimated Measurements→ Beta Fit Grouping→ Recommendations

A standard A4 sheet gives the system a known reference size in the frame. Once your foot is correctly placed and the image is captured, the app corrects for camera angle and estimates length and width. Those estimates feed a beta fit grouping and a set of practical recommendations.

Journey Two

Fit Check.

02
Problem Area→ Sensation→ Timing & Shoe Context→ Safety Check→ Rule-Based Plan

No scan needed. You describe where it hurts, what it feels like, and when it happens in a specific shoe. The app checks your answers against a set of safety flags, then returns a plain-language plan of what to try, what to avoid and what to monitor.

Neither journey performs a medical diagnosis. Both produce estimates and educational guidance based on the information you provide.
