Why Fit Matters

Footwear can feel wrong for more than one reason.

A shoe can feel wrong because of length, width, shape, volume, or how it holds the foot during movement. A size label alone rarely explains the whole picture.

Badger Fit is being designed to help people recognise those differences earlier, before they keep guessing or buying the wrong pair again.
