Frequently Asked Questions

Still deciding where to begin?

Everything you need to know about scanning, Fit Check and your data—kept simple.

What is BadgerFit?
A foot-scan and shoe Fit Check tool that turns measurements or shoe-specific answers into practical, rule-based guidance.
Should I scan or use Fit Check?
Scan to build an estimated foot profile. Use Fit Check when one particular shoe feels uncomfortable and you want a focused plan.
What do I need for the scan?
One A4 sheet, a phone camera, suitable lighting and a BadgerFit account.
Is BadgerFit medical advice?
No. It provides educational fit and footwear guidance. Persistent, severe or unusual discomfort should be assessed by an appropriate healthcare professional.
What is a Badger Cluster?
A beta grouping based on available width and arch information. Its boundaries are still being evaluated.
How do recommendations work?
Rules connect your scan results or Fit Check answers to practical suggestions and relevant education. Some product links may be affiliate links and will be clearly identified.
Are scan images stored?
Successful scan images are processed temporarily and are not retained. Failed-scan diagnostic information is stored only with separate opt-in consent.
